It's our top 10 Do's and Don'ts. The first is don't trust anything. We've talked about this a number of times throughout the course. Don't assume that whatever the user is inputting is valid. Do expect that hackers are going to find your API endpoints, even those undocumented ones. Don't fall for the myth that APIs are hidden, they can't be discovered, and you don't need to worry about them. Just remember all those breaches that we talked about earlier with those APIs that were left totally wide open. Don't have hidden or unadvertised features.
