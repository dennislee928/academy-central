


## SOC Overview

The SOC team's goal is to detect, analyze, and respond to cybersecurity incidents using a combination of technology solutions and a set of processes to help mitigate the incidents.

The SOC (also known as Computer Emergency Response Teams and Computer Security Incident Response Teams) is a team of security professionals that are dedicated to monitoring and analyzing activity on networks, servers, endpoints, databases, applications, websites, and other systems that connect to your network either locally or from a remote location.

## Security Operations

Security Operations (SecOps) is a collaborative effort between security teams and operations teams that integrates tools, processes, and technology for protecting our digital way of life.

## SOC Objectives and Strategies

The first step to creating an organization's SOC is to clearly define the business-specific objectives of various departments and a strategy that includes input and support from managers. After the strategy is documented, the infrastructure required to support this strategy should be implemented by choosing the required technology and tools.

The following are objectives and strategies of a SOC.

### Planning  
  

---

Setup of a SOC requires careful planning, and physical safety must be taken into consideration. The layout of the operation center also must be carefully designed to be both comfortable and functional. Lighting and acoustic issues should not be ignored. The SOC can contain many areas such as the operation room, a war room, and the offices of the supervisors. Comfort, visibility, efficiency, and control are important terms in this scenario, and each area should be designed according to those factors.

### Analysis  
  

---

The most important benefit of having a SOC is the improvement of detection of security incidents through continuous monitoring and analysis of data effectiveness. As an organization analyzes data events across its networks, endpoints, servers, and databases throughout the day, SOC teams are critical for ensuring timely detection and response of security incidents.

### Efficiency  
  

---

Successful SOCs also leverage threat intelligence and security automation and orchestration technologies to be effective and efficient. Organizations are increasing their analytical power to expand security measures and provide better defense against data breaches and cyberattacks by combining highly skilled security analysts and these technologies.
